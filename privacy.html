OU Study Planner

Privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

Your study plan is kept on your own device, and in your own Google Drive if you turn sync on. There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking, and nobody else can see your data, including me.

What's stored

Your modules, assessments, tutorials, shifts, blocked time, study blocks and settings. These are saved in your browser's storage on your device and don't go anywhere else unless you turn on sync or save a backup file yourself.

Google Drive sync

Sync is off until you turn it on. When it's on, the app saves a file called study-planner.json in an OU Study Planner folder in your Google Drive so your devices can share it. It uses Google's drive.file permission, which only lets the app see files it created, not anything else in your Drive.

The sign in token from Google is kept in your browser and lasts about an hour. You can remove the app's access any time in your Google account under third-party apps with account access. Deleting the OU Study Planner folder removes the synced copy.

Screenshots

If you add a tutorial from a screenshot, it's read on your device and isn't uploaded or kept.

Hosting

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub only sends the app to your browser and never sees your study data, but like any website it keeps normal server logs such as IP addresses. These are covered by GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Clear the site data in your browser or uninstall the app to remove everything from that device. Delete the OU Study Planner folder in Google Drive to remove the synced copy.

Questions

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.

OU Study Planner isn't affiliated with or endorsed by The Open University.
